The short version

What the best videos have in common

1

Win the first 2 seconds

Our best videos keep 65–80% of viewers past 2 seconds. The average is 49%. Open with your verdict, your face, or a question, never a slow intro.

2

Say who you are

"As a doctor…", "As a medical mom…". A one-line reason to trust you is the strongest opener we see.

3

Answer a real question

Replies to comments and "honest reviews" convert best because they tackle the doubt a parent has before buying.

4

Show it, then prove it

A bold claim gets taps; the label, your child taking it, and the price close the sale.

Steal this

A 30-second recipe that works

  1. 0–2s
    Hook. Your verdict, a question from a comment, or who you are. Product visible.
  2. 2–10s
    Why you. Your child, your routine, the problem you were solving.
  3. 10–20s
    Show it. Your child taking it, the taste reaction, the label up close.
  4. 20–30s
    Close. Price or promo, and "tap the yellow basket". Say it out loud.
Keep it safe

Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Share your own child's experience, in your own words
  • Say Wisey Lion "supports" brain development and focus
  • Show the product and its label clearly
  • Follow TikTok's rules for showing a paid or affiliate post

Don't

  • Say it cures, treats or prevents any condition
  • Promise results ("guaranteed smarter", "in 7 days")
  • Use medical before-and-after claims
  • Copy another creator's video without Stitching or crediting them
